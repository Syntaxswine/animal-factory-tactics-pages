Every variant, magnified

Six tuft designs and six undergrowth designs, drawn far above their on-screen size. In play a tuft is 5.6 to 9.0 px across and 3.4 to 6.7 px tall; a clump is 16.2 to 23.2 across and 18.9 to 39.4 tall. The middle clump of every woodland tile takes the pale tone, which is the one piece of the 3D branch's material assignment that survives into a painted sprite.

Where the numbers come from

Placement
foliage-models.js on project/tactics-3d, hash for hash: yard one tuft on a third of its tiles, grass and woodland two, woodland three undergrowth clumps.
Height scale
One tile unit lifts 28·√2·cos 30° = 34.29 px, which is the 3D branch's own hybrid projection, so waist high there is waist high here.
Palette
Measured off the artwork rather than chosen: bush.png averages #6a6135 in its top-left quadrant and #5d5530 in its bottom-right, and the commonest tones in ground-grass.png run #473b24 to #756937.
Mechanics
None. Woodland already costs nine tiles of optical distance per tile crossed; this parcel adds no cover, no collision and no sight cost.
