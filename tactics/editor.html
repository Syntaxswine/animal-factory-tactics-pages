DESIGN WORKSPACE

Full map:100 sectors, three levels.

SAVED DESIGNS

Place replaces the sector at X/Y on all three levels. Undo restores it. Blocks store terrain, edges, props, guards and climbs; squad starts and travel markers belong to full maps. Saved designs stay on this browser and device.

PLACE BLOCKS

Drag from a tile boundary to draw a straight wall line. The starting edge locks its direction. Release to apply; Escape cancels.

GENERATE A STARTING MAP

Generated maps have four squad starts and 46 guards, across 100 sectors of 24 × 24 tiles. River layouts match sector connections and require two open bridges and their approach roads. Generation and template changes can be undone.

KEEP YOUR MAP

Drafts stay in this browser. Export a file for a portable backup.
