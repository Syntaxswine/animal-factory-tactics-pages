    WORKERS’ FIELD MANUAL / 01

    Four comrades. One factory.

    Defeat the guards in each local map. Your squad is tougher and more accurate, but open ground is dangerous. Explore at your own pace; spotting an opponent starts turn-based combat immediately.

    Move, aim, act

    Select a squad card or press 1–4. Click open ground to walk. Click any tile, including unexplored ground, to set a destination. Routes through unknown terrain are tentative: the character moves toward the point and replans when obstacles become visible. New enemy contact, exhausted AP or Escape stops movement. Hover to preview the estimated route and cost. During combat, standing movement costs 2 AP per tile, kneeling 4, and prone 8. Diagonal steps cost1.5×:3 AP standing,6 kneeling,12 prone. Both adjoining tiles and all four corner edges must be clear. Any change to a different stance costs 2 AP, including standing directly to prone. Stance changes are free outside combat. Stand before using stairs, ladders or roof climbs; stairs cost 2 AP per level and ladders cost 3. Marked roof edges (gold R arrows) cost 6 AP up or down and connect one level only. Stand on a cyan stair marker and use the climb ↑ or ↓ buttons. Movement stops on first contact. Use Escape to stop a route.

    Click a guard or their contact button, inspect hit chance and cover, then press Attack / F. End the squad turn with Space after using all four characters. Guards then move and fire. Clear the alerted guards to resume exploration.

    The test armoury

    Everyone can equip all five weapons. Switching costs 2 AP in combat; reloading costs 3. Each weapon keeps its own magazine. Spare ammunition is unlimited. The AK-47 has a three-round burst (6 AP). Hands and the NR-40 require an adjacent tile.

    Height and terrain

    Gun range uses horizontal distance plus 1 tile per level uphill. Firing downhill uses normal range with no penalty or bonus. Clear terrain can be seen up to75tiles away; characters can be detected up to60tiles. These are maximum distances, and walls and floors still block sight. Directional sight cones are planned for the next stage. Shooting uphill applies a 15-point cover penalty; stronger 25-point prop cover takes precedence. Solid floors and walls still block shots. Water cannot be walked through; use bridge decks. Fences stop movement but let you see and fire through. Windows allow rays through a simplified central opening; decorative mullions do not affect shots, while sills and side walls remain solid. Closed-door sprites are fixed barriers in this test; the open doorway is passable.

    Read the factory

    Walls sit between tiles and block crossing, sight and fire; doorways are open edge passages. Crates block walking and provide cover from their side (−25 hit chance). Dark ground is unexplored; dim ground is remembered. Guards appear only while visible. Red rings identify opponents, gold your selected worker, green your other workers.

    Right-drag or arrows to pan. Wheel or +/− to zoom. C centers your selected worker. G toggles the grid. Local maps are 240 × 240 tiles, with 100 sectors of 24 × 24 and three walkable levels. Use the level selector to focus a floor; lower layers are dark and upper layers pale. Map clicks affect the focused layer. Use it to inspect each floor, sector coordinates to navigate, and Overview to see the full map. Double-click the overview to inspect a sector. Solid upper floors block shots; stair openings and platform edges can permit cross-level sight. The blue travel marker connects this local map to the overmap. Gather every living member within 2 tiles outside combat to travel. Cleared maps remain walkable. Restart resets all local maps in this run. No saves or healing in this mechanics test.
